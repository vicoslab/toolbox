Tour: Training

On this page you can configure options for model training. Individual options may differ between models, so you should review their descriptions by hovering over. In any case, a dataset manifest is needed to provide information about training samples to the training procedure. If you came here from the 'Export' step, the dataset manifest should be filled in for you already. Otherwise you can find past exports on the status page.

For your own dataset, you are expected to prepare a dataset manifest yourself and manually enter the path for the dataset manifest in the options. Rewiew the datasets page to check what formatting is required.
