{{ brand_name_long }} is a collection of tools which is designed to aid the development and practical application of deep learning approaches. The mixture of available tools ranges from state-of-the-art models developed by our researchers, to openly available models and tools, which aid in the development and adaptation of our models to new problems.

Steps

Model selection

Select a model based on your use case.

Prepare dataset

Collect a diverse set of representative images.

Annotation

Annotate images with labels (depending on the model, this may be a mask, points, ...). If you use external tools, you will need to create a file with information about the dataset (follow guidelines for your selected model).

Training

Train model based on your annotations to obtain weights.

Monitoring

Monitor training metrics for issues.

Evaluation

Run inference on unseen examples and inspect incorrect results.

Iterate

Collect additional samples or correct annotations if necessary, and repeat previous steps to obtain new weights.

Finish

Deploy inference worker or download annotations and training artifacts from the toolbox.
