Tour: Inference

The inference tab is where you start inference workers based on pretrained weights or training artifacts. If you came here from the training/monitoring step, the weights may be filled in already, otherwise you need to add them from the options. Then you can start the inference worker. The other options are the same as with training, but are not relevant unless specified otherwise. This is the final step. Apply the trained model and review results by uploading samples into the interface. Depending on the task, interaction may be required.
